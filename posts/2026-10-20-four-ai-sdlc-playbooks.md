4 companies published their take on the AI-native software lifecycle in the last few months: Anthropic, Atlassian, Google Cloud and Shopify.

I read them side by side.

They agree on more than I expected. Writing code is no longer the bottleneck. Review is. And the quality of the context you give an agent matters more than which model you pick.

Where they split is more useful. Should the source of truth live in the repo or in a platform graph? One standard agent tool, or many? Hard blocking guardrails, or alerts that keep experiments alive?

Those are three decisions every organisation will have to make on purpose.

What surprised me most: none of the four publishes a methodology behind its numbers. Shopify's engineering head has even said publicly that its 20% productivity figure was his own rough estimate.

So I put my notes, the full comparison and a 16-point self-assessment in an open repo. The short version is a 9-page carousel.
